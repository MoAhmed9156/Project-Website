🔬 THE PROTOTYPE

CLOSED-LOOP FEEDBACK CONTROL

Our AquaCore system implements a closed-loop feedback control system that monitors three crucial environmental parameters: 1) Temperature, 2) Light Intensity, and 3) Water Flow Rate. The system automatically activates actuators (heater, cooling fans, water pump, and lighting system) to maintain optimal conditions for the selected aquatic species without any human interaction.

SOLVING EGYPT'S GRAND CHALLENGES

🌾

AGRICULTURAL BASE

Egypt faces water scarcity with only 570 m³ per capita annually. AquaCore reduces water usage by 90% compared to traditional aquaculture, enabling sustainable fish production in arid regions. With Egypt's population exceeding 100 million, the demand for food continues to rise while water resources remain limited.

🏭

POLLUTION REDUCTION

Traditional fish farms discharge nutrient-rich waste into the Nile, causing eutrophication. Our closed-loop system captures 100% of waste for biogas production and organic fertilizer, addressing pollution fouling our air, water, and soil.

👥

POPULATION GROWTH

Egypt is the most populated country in the Middle East and one of the fastest growing populations. This project directly tackles food security challenges by providing a portable, cost-effective, and automated solution for small-scale aquaculture.

♻️

RECYCLED MATERIALS

The prototype body is constructed from recycled materials, demonstrating how garbage and waste can be utilized for economic and environmental purposes, aligning with Egypt's sustainability goals.

TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS

Tank Volume: 10 Liters
Max Fish Capacity: 4 Tilapia (20g each)
Power Consumption: 235W (Maximum)
Water Recycling Rate: 95% Closed Loop
IoT Protocol: MQTT over WiFi (ESP32)
Data Update Interval: 4 Seconds
Microcontroller: ESP32 (520 KB SRAM)
Operating Duration: 6 Consecutive Hours
